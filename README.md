## Career and Employer Research Project

## Description

This project was completed for my BAIS:3050 Professional Preparation course at the University of Iowa. The purpose was to research potential career opportunities and better understand the responsibilities, qualifications, and expectations of different positions.

I researched two internship opportunities at Athene: Business System Analyst Intern and Product Marketing Intern. For each position, I examined the company background, job responsibilities, required qualifications, and how my education and experience relate to the role.

## Tools Used

- **LinkedIn** - Finding and researching internship opportunities
- **Company Websites** - Researching employer backgrounds and company information
- **Microsoft Word** - Organizing and documenting research
- **PDF** - Saving and sharing completed research

## Files Used

- **Employer Job Posting Research #1.pdf** - Research on Athene's Business System Analyst Intern position.
- **Employer Job Posting Research #2.pdf** - Research on Athene's Product Marketing Intern position.
